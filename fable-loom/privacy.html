Fable Loom Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Fable Loom does not collect, store or send any personal information. There are no accounts, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Fables you save to your shelf, and any hero name you type, are kept only on your device so you can read them again. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing

When you choose Share, the fable’s text is handed to your device’s share sheet, and you decide where it goes. Fable Loom does not see or keep what you share.

Read aloud

Read aloud uses your device’s built-in speech voice. The story text is not sent to us.

Children

Fable Loom is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions can be sent through the support link on the App Store page.
