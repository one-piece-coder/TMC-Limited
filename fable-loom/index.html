    About The Fable Loom

    The Fable Loom weaves a new fable each time you ask: a flawed hero, a grand quest, trials that test them, and a moral with a lesson you can use in everyday life. Every story and window is made on your device. The app works without an internet connection.

    Privacy

    The Fable Loom collects no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Fables you save stay on this device and are never sent anywhere. Names you type are used only to tell the story. Sharing a fable happens only when you choose Share, through your device’s own share sheet.
